1. Warehouse management: Audit the daily management of the warehouse, from opening to closing.

2. Staff management: Verify the process for hiring/terminating and managing warehouse and shop staff

3. Staff Movement: Audit employee movement within duty-free areas, including security measures, access controls, and adherence to standard operating procedures.

4. IT system: Audit supporting IT system (e.g., ERP)

1. Storage Management: Audit the storage procedures, including inventory management, stocktaking, and adherence to customs regulations.

2. Stock counting: Verifying items within the systems (ERP and DF-IMS) are matching with the actual stocks present in the warehouse and the shop, this includes going through selected good's HS codes (e.g., perfumes and testers) and quantities

1. Point of Sale (POS): Audit the system usage and configuration

2. Goods sold: Verify items sold are compliant with policies (e.g., restricted products)

3. Sales Registration: Verify the procedures linked to sales, from documentation being checked (e.g., passport, boarding pass, etc.) to goods handling (e.g., sealing)

4. Signage: Validate proper signage and information regarding allowance are provided to travelers

5. Special cases: Sales procedures to transit passengers, handling of returns and exchanges, etc.

6. Security: Verifying security measures in the shop including surveillance systems, access control, and anti-theft measures

1. Documentation Review: Review all gathered documentation and audit findings for accuracy and completeness.

2. Prepare Audit Report: Create a comprehensive audit report, including the audit objectives, scope, findings, recommendations, and any non-compliance issues identified.

3. Submit Report: Submit the audit report to the operator, highlighting all violations, penalties and timeframe for resolution

4. Verification: Verify compliance after timeframe for resolution is up and potentially apply new penalties
