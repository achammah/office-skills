Incident management

LOB and Shared Services

  • ■Recovery plans are developed by both groups using a standardized plan and method-ology by tech-risk
  • ■Plans are changed by business group and technology as necessary.
  • ■Submits business demand vs. recovery capabilities to tech-risk
  • ■Provides business demands to technology
  • ■Performs annual tests of business recovery plans or applications
  • ■Alerts “Help Desk” who alerts technology of incident

Technology

  • ■Business group approves the plan
  • ■Enables recovery of system through investing resources
  • ■Performs root-cause analysis on failed components of tests
  • ■Tech executes recovery plan as needed

Oversight

  • ■Approves plan sent by business group
  • ■Performs annual review of changes to mission critical systems
  • ■Analyzes demand across org to finalize common recovery objectives across systems.
  • ■Aggregates recovery objectives and gives them to technology for enablement
  • ■Aggregates results and chairs BC Steering Committee
  • ■Determines level of engagement for severity level 1 incidents
  • ■Technology works with business group on recovery plan

Key metrics

  • ■Y Business Processes

  • ■X business processes with alternate work area (AWA) risk identified
  • ■X AWA remediated
  • ■X Business recovery (AWA) plans tested
  • ■Y “table-top” exercises
  • ■X total incidents (to be broken down further after mtg with Technology)
