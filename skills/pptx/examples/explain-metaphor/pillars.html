95% of enterprise AI pilots fail due to execution gaps, not technology

42% of companies abandoned most AI initiatives in 2025 (up from 17% in 2024). This is an execution failure, not a technology failure.

Business-Tech Misalignment

AI requires deep workflow integration, not standalone tools. Organizations are 2x more likely to succeed when they redesign workflows before selecting AI. This is a lean management transformation, not a software rollout.

Internal Teams Slowness

Large enterprises take 9 months to scale vs. 90 days for agile firms. Internal builds succeed only 33% of the time compared to 67% with specialized partners. Technology evolves faster than enterprise IT can adapt.

Talent Shortage

AI talent shortage jumped from 28% to 51% in 18 months. 87% of organizations struggle to hire AI developers with 142 days average to fill roles. Hybrid profiles with both tech and business skills are exceptionally rare.
