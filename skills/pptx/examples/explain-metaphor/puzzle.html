Strong influencing skills build understanding and conviction, a key part of the four-part Influence Model to change mindsets

“I will change my own behavior if…”

Role-modeling

“I see superiors, peers and subordinates behaving in the new way”

Fostering understanding and conviction

“I know what is expected of me – I agree with it, and it is meaningful”

Developing talent and skills

“I have the skills and competencies to behave in the new way”

Reinforcing with formal mechanisms

“The structures, processes and systems reinforce the change in behavior I am being asked to make”
